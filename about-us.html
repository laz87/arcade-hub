About Us

GameLet is a simple, joyful hub for Swahili–English word games. Our mission is to help learners and gamers practice vocabulary, spelling, and problem-solving using playful, accessible mini‑games.

Our Mission

  • Make Swahili learning fun and approachable for everyone.
  • Celebrate Swahili and English through engaging, browser-based games.
  • Keep the experience lightweight, fast, and free to play.

What We Build

We design and curate quick-play experiences: word search, vocabulary quizzes, spelling challenges, and more. We aim for clean design, smooth performance, and mobile-friendly play sessions.
